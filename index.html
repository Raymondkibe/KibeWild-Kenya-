🏖️ Coastal Gems: Mombasa & Malindi

Beyond the savannah, Kenya's Indian Ocean coastline offers white sand, turquoise waters, and rich Swahili culture.

Mombasa

Kenya's oldest coastal city, Mombasa blends history and relaxation. Visit Fort Jesus (UNESCO), explore Old Town's Swahili architecture, and unwind on Diani Beach – consistently ranked among Africa's best. Enjoy fresh seafood, vibrant night markets, and marine parks perfect for snorkeling and diving.

Diani Beach – one of Africa's best

Malindi

A laid-back paradise north of Mombasa, Malindi is famous for the Malindi Marine National Park – a protected reef with sea turtles, dolphins, and colorful coral. Visit the Vasco da Gama Pillar, watch traditional dhow sailing, and experience Watamu's Gede Ruins nearby. Ideal for kite surfing and deep-sea fishing.

Malindi Marine National Park

🌍 Kenya's Environmental Heroes

From Nobel laureates to grassroots defenders — these champions protect our land and wildlife.

Wangari Maathai

Nobel Peace Prize 2004 | Founder, Green Belt Movement

First African woman to win Nobel Prize. Planted over 51 million trees, empowered women, and fought for democracy.

Truphena Muthoni

72‑hour tree hugger | Youth climate activist

Planted over 11,000 trees. Her viral tree‑hugging protest inspired global action against deforestation.

Phyllis Omido

Goldman Environmental Prize 2015

Led fight against lead poisoning in Mombasa, shut down toxic smelter, saved thousands of children.

Kivutha Kibwana

Former Makueni Governor

Pioneered community water conservation and sustainable agriculture policies.

James Wakibia

Plastic pollution fighter

Key campaigner behind Kenya's world-famous plastic bag ban (2017).

Paula Kahumbu

CEO, WildlifeDirect

“Hands Off Our Elephants” campaign, global voice for elephant protection.

Elizabeth Wathuti

Founder, Green Generation Initiative

Youth climate activist, planted thousands of trees, addressed UN summits.

Richard Leakey

Former KWS Director

Revolutionized anti-poaching, saved Kenya's elephants and rhinos from extinction.

Wanjira Mathai

World Resources Institute

Continues Wangari's legacy, leads global forest restoration programs.
